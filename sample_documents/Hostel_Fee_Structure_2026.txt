NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
STUDENT RESIDENCE & HOSTEL REGULATIONS (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

1. HOSTEL ACCOMMODATION & ROOM CATEGORIES
The college provides on-campus residential facilities across 4 Boys Hostels (Aryabhata, Ramanujan, Kalam, Bhabha) and 3 Girls Hostels (Gargi, Kalpana, Sarojini).

Annual Room Rent & Maintenance Fees:
- Single Occupancy (Air Conditioned, Attached Bath): ₹75,000 per academic year
- Double Occupancy (Air Conditioned): ₹55,000 per academic year
- Triple Occupancy (Non-AC, Standard): ₹45,000 per academic year
- Four Sharing (Non-AC, Economy): ₹35,000 per academic year

Refundable Hostel Security Caution Deposit: ₹10,000 (charged once during initial hostel allotment).

2. MESS AND DINING CHARGES
Mess membership is compulsory for all resident students.
- Annual Mess Fee: ₹42,000 per year (covers 4 meals daily: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, Dinner).
- Special festive dinner and weekend special meals are included without extra cost.
- Rebate on mess fees is applicable if a student is absent for more than 7 consecutive days with prior written approval from the Chief Warden.

3. HOSTEL TIMINGS AND CURFEW RULES
- Main campus and hostel entrance gates are closed at 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on Saturdays and Sundays.
- Night out pass requests must be submitted through the student portal 24 hours prior and approved by parent/guardian.
- Cooking inside student rooms is strictly prohibited and attracts a penalty of ₹5,000.
