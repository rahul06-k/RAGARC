NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
ACADEMIC EVALUATION & EXAMINATION REGULATIONS (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

1. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
A student must secure a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered theory and practical course to be eligible to appear for the End-Semester Examinations.
- Condonation for attendance between 65% and 74.9% may be granted solely on valid medical grounds, accompanied by a registered doctor's certificate and approved by the Dean of Academic Affairs.
- Students with attendance below 65% in a course are marked 'De-registered' (Grade 'F') and must repeat the course during subsequent summer or regular semesters.

2. GRADING SYSTEM AND GPA CALCULATION
The institute follows a 10-point Letter Grading System:
- Grade O (Outstanding): 10 grade points (Marks >= 90%)
- Grade A+ (Excellent): 9 grade points (Marks 80% to 89%)
- Grade A (Very Good): 8 grade points (Marks 70% to 79%)
- Grade B+ (Good): 7 grade points (Marks 60% to 69%)
- Grade B (Above Average): 6 grade points (Marks 50% to 59%)
- Grade C (Average / Pass): 5 grade points (Marks 40% to 49%)
- Grade F (Fail): 0 grade points (Marks < 40%)

The Minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) required for the award of the B.Tech degree is 5.00 with no active backlogs.

3. RE-EVALUATION AND SUPPLEMENTARY EXAMS
- Students seeking re-evaluation of answer scripts can apply within 10 calendar days of result declaration with a fee of ₹500 per paper.
- Supplementary examinations are held annually in July for students with backlogs.
