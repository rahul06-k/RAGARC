NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
CAREER DEVELOPMENT & PLACEMENT CELL POLICY (BATCH 2026-2027)

1. STUDENT ELIGIBILITY & REGISTRATION
All graduating final-year students with a minimum CGPA of 6.0 and no active backlogs are eligible to register with the Training & Placement (T&P) Cell.
Mandatory registration deadline is August 31 of the pre-final year.

2. ONE-STUDENT ONE-OFFER POLICY AND DREAM OFFERS
To ensure equal career opportunities for all students, the institute enforces the "One Student One Job Offer" guideline with specific exceptions for Dream and Super Dream companies:
- Standard Offer: Any company offering CTC up to ₹7.5 LPA.
- Dream Offer: Companies offering CTC between ₹7.5 LPA and ₹15 LPA. A student holding a standard offer is eligible to participate in up to 3 Dream Company drives.
- Super Dream Offer: Companies offering CTC above ₹15 LPA. Any placed student is permitted to appear for Super Dream opportunities until securing one Super Dream offer.

3. CODE OF CONDUCT AND DISCIPLINARY ACTIONS
- Attendance is compulsory for all shortlisted candidates in pre-placement talks (PPTs), coding assessments, and technical interviews.
- Unexcused absence after shortlisting will lead to immediate de-registration from the T&P Cell for 60 days.
- Forging resumes or submitting false CGPA/academic data will result in permanent debarment from campus recruitment and disciplinary action before the Institute Ethics Board.
