NATIONAL INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING & TECHNOLOGY
OFFICIAL ADMISSIONS BROCHURE & ELIGIBILITY GUIDELINES (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)

1. UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSIONS (B.TECH)
The National Institute of Engineering & Technology offers four-year Bachelor of Technology programs across multiple disciplines. 
Total B.Tech Intake for 2026:
- Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): 180 seats
- Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS): 120 seats
- Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE): 120 seats
- Mechanical Engineering (ME): 60 seats
- Civil Engineering (CE): 60 seats

Eligibility Criteria:
Candidates must have passed 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics as mandatory subjects with an aggregate score of at least 60% (55% for SC/ST candidates). Valid JEE Main or State CET rank is mandatory for all admissions under the merit quota.

Important Admission Dates for 2026:
- Application portal opens: April 15, 2026
- Application deadline: June 30, 2026
- Counseling Round 1: July 10, 2026
- Counseling Round 2: July 22, 2026
- Commencement of Classes: August 16, 2026

2. TUITION FEES AND REFUND POLICY
The annual tuition fee for B.Tech programs is ₹1,35,000 per academic year, payable in two semester installments.
Laboratory and library deposit (one-time, refundable) is ₹10,000.
Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) is granted to the top 5% ranked admitted students whose parental income is below ₹6 Lakhs per annum.

Withdrawal and Refund Rules:
- If withdrawal is requested before the commencement of classes, 100% of the tuition fee minus a processing charge of ₹1,000 will be refunded.
- If withdrawal occurs within 15 days after classes commence, 80% of the tuition fee is refundable.
- No tuition refunds are entertained after 30 days of the commencement of the academic term.
